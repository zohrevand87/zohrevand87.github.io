About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Tehran, supervised by Dr. Reshad Hosseini and Prof. Hadi Moradi. My research has spanned handwritten and document analysis, biometrics, remote-sensing image understanding, and deep learning. Alongside research, I have more than eight years of university teaching experience.
