01 — Research profile

Probabilistic models for a changing world.

I develop machine-learning methods that represent uncertainty and dependency in complex data. My current work focuses on probabilistic and generative models for sequence and time-series analysis, informed by earlier research in computer vision, document analysis, biometrics, and remote sensing.
